Zombie Hunter Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Summary

Zombie Hunter is a free game supported by ads. We do not ask you to create an account, and the game does not collect your name, email address, contact list, photos, microphone or location. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google's advertising and billing services collect, described below.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, settings and whether you removed ads in the app's private storage on your phone. This data is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, device information (such as model and operating system version) and information about how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions that require it, the game shows Google's consent form on first launch and you can change your choice later from the game menu. You can reset or disable your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). See how Google uses data from ads and Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that the purchase was made; we never see your payment details.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone except the Google services described above.

Children

Zombie Hunter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
